# Nexora Tech — Employee Handbook (Sample)

> This is a fictional company handbook created only for testing the RAG system.

## 1. Working Hours

Nexora Tech's standard working hours are 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Sunday to Thursday.
Core hours, when every employee must be available for meetings, are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Friday and Saturday are weekly holidays.

## 2. Remote Work Policy

Employees who have completed their 3-month probation may work from home up to
2 days per week. Remote days must be logged in the HR portal ("NexoraHub") by
Wednesday of the previous week. Team leads can deny a remote day if an
on-site client meeting is scheduled.

Employees on probation must work from the office every day.

## 3. Leave Policy

| Leave type      | Days per year | Notes                                              |
| --------------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Annual leave    | 20            | Up to 5 unused days can be carried to next year    |
| Sick leave      | 14            | A doctor's note is required for more than 2 days   |
| Casual leave    | 10            | Must be applied at least 1 day in advance          |
| Parental leave  | 120 (mother) / 15 (father) | Fully paid                            |

Annual leave longer than 5 consecutive working days needs approval from the
department head at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 4. Salary and Bonus

Salary is paid on the last working day of every month via bank transfer.
Employees receive two festival bonuses per year (Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha),
each equal to 50% of the basic salary. A performance bonus of up to 2 months'
basic salary is paid every January based on the annual review score.

## 5. Learning Budget

Every full-time employee gets a yearly learning budget of 30,000 BDT for
courses, books, or conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.
To claim, submit the receipt in NexoraHub within 30 days of purchase.

## 6. Equipment

New engineers receive a laptop (16 GB RAM minimum) and a 27-inch monitor.
Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported
to IT (it-support@nexora.example) within 24 hours.

## 7. Code of Conduct

Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. Report concerns to hr@nexora.example
or anonymously through the "Speak Up" form in NexoraHub. All reports are
investigated within 7 working days.
