CloudBox by Nexora Tech — Product FAQ (Sample)
(Fictional product, created only for testing the RAG system.)

Q: What is CloudBox?
A: CloudBox is Nexora Tech's file storage and sharing service for small businesses.
It lets teams store files, share links, and collaborate on documents from any device.

Q: What plans are available?
A: There are three plans:
- Free: 5 GB storage, 1 user, share links expire after 7 days.
- Team: 1 TB storage, up to 10 users, 500 BDT per user per month.
- Business: 5 TB storage, unlimited users, 900 BDT per user per month, includes audit logs and SSO.

Q: Is there a maximum file size?
A: On the Free plan the maximum single file size is 250 MB. On Team and Business plans it is 10 GB.

Q: How long are deleted files kept?
A: Deleted files stay in the Trash for 30 days on Free and Team plans, and 180 days on the Business plan.
After that they are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: All data is stored in data centers in Singapore, with an encrypted backup copy in Frankfurt.
Files are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Free plan users can use the community forum. Team plan users get email support
(support@cloudbox.example) with a 24-hour response time. Business plan users get
24/7 phone support at +880-1700-000000 and a 2-hour response time.

Q: Can I cancel anytime?
A: Yes. Plans are billed monthly and you can cancel anytime from Settings > Billing.
No refunds are given for partial months.
